Tech

More technical details about our CanSat and its ground station.

Data Path

During the flight Cansat's sensors send readings to the onboard controller(MCU). Then MCU sends them by radio to the ground station, which adds introduces second part and passes them over Wi-Fi to the dashboard for live viewing.

  1. Sensors
  2. RP2350(Cansat's MCU)
  3. ESP32(Ground Station's MCU)
  4. PC

Cansat as well as Ground station and PC receiving data all write timestamped readings to local storage. Such redundancy used to ensure maximum data preservation with further analysis on losses(during which stsage data was lost)

Wifi will be hosted from any available device, acces to net is not required, but if available, it will be used to send data to the cloud directly for further analysis.

What each part does

Sensors
Measure the air, motion, position, and sound during flight.
RP2350
The small computer inside the CanSat that collects and timestamps readings.
Local storage
Keeps a copy of the readings inside the CanSat for later analysis.
LoRa
The radio link between the CanSat and the ground.
ESP32 ground station
Receives readings from the radio and relays them to the computer over Wi-Fi.
Dashboard
The page on the computer that plots the readings live.

Measurements & Hardware

Atmosphere
BME688 for pressure, temperature, humidity, and gas resistance. TMP36 and SHT45 provide additional temperature and humidity readings. A dedicated CO sensor is planned.
Motion and position
An IMU measures acceleration and rotation. The MAX-M10S GPS logs position and speed so we can reconstruct the flight path.
Power and sound
We’re considering an INA260 to monitor voltage and current, and an ICS-43434 microphone to capture flight sounds.

Software & Storage

The flight software will timestamp readings and log them to microSD. We’re also considering onboard flash storage.

After the flight, we can calculate quantities such as descent speed from changes in altitude over time and compare the results with the radio recording.

Electronics & CAD

The payload design brings the RP2350, sensors, storage, and radio together inside a can-sized enclosure.

We’ll add wiring diagrams and interactive 3D models when the files are ready.
