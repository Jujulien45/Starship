Tech

Inside the CanSat and its ground station, from the first sensor reading to the flight plots.

Data Path

  1. Sensors
  2. RP2350
  3. LoRa link
  4. ESP32 ground station
  5. Wi-Fi to dashboard

The onboard controller also writes timestamped readings to local storage so we can analyze the flight after recovery. The radio model is still under consideration.

Measurements & Hardware

Atmosphere
BME688 for pressure, temperature, humidity, and gas resistance. TMP36 and SHT45 provide additional temperature and humidity readings. A dedicated CO sensor is planned.
Motion and position
An IMU measures acceleration and rotation. The MAX-M10S GPS logs position and speed so we can reconstruct the flight path.
Power and sound
We’re considering an INA260 to monitor voltage and current, and an ICS-43434 microphone to capture flight sounds.

Software & Storage

The flight software will timestamp readings and log them to microSD. We’re also considering onboard flash storage. After the flight, we can calculate quantities such as descent speed from changes in altitude over time and compare the results with the radio recording.

Electronics & CAD

The payload design brings the RP2350, sensors, storage, and radio together inside a can-sized enclosure. We’ll add wiring diagrams and interactive 3D models when the files are ready.
