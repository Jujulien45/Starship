Tech

More technical details about our CanSat and its ground station.

Data Path

During flight, the CanSat’s RP2350 collects sensor readings and sends them by radio to the ESP32 ground station. The station adds its own sensor readings and forwards both sets over Wi-Fi to the PC for live viewing.

The CanSat, ground station, and PC each save timestamped readings locally. Such redundancy in the logs after flight helps us recover missing readings and identify where data was lost.

Any available device(e.g. phone) can host the local Wi-Fi network. The dashboard can be hosted on local pc and function offline, but if available, internet connection lets us send data directly to the cloud.

What each part does

Sensors
On board modules that record measurements such as: atmospheric conditions, motion, position, and sound during flight.
RP2350(MCU)
The CanSat’s microcontroller. It collects sensor readings and timestamps them.
LoRa
The radio technology that carries readings from the CanSat to the ground station.
ESP32(MCU) ground station
The ESP32 is the station’s microcontroller. It adds ground measurements to the flight readings and forwards both over Wi-Fi.
PC and dashboard
The PC saves the readings locally and runs the dashboard that plots them live.

Measurements & Hardware

Atmosphere
BME688 for pressure, temperature, humidity, and gas resistance. TMP36 and SHT45 provide additional temperature and humidity readings. A dedicated CO sensor is planned.
Motion and position
An IMU measures acceleration and rotation. The MAX-M10S GPS logs position and speed so we can reconstruct the flight path.
Power and sound
We’re considering an INA260 to monitor voltage and current, and an ICS-43434 microphone to capture flight sounds.

Software & Storage

The flight software will timestamp readings and log them to microSD. We’re also considering onboard flash storage.

After the flight, we can calculate quantities such as descent speed from changes in altitude over time and compare the results with the radio recording.

Electronics & CAD

The payload design brings the RP2350, sensors, storage, and radio together inside a can-sized enclosure.

We’ll add wiring diagrams and interactive 3D models when the files are ready.
