Engineering

The systems that take a measurement from the payload to the ground station.

Mission Objectives

Collect flight measurements and make them available for review after recovery.

Hardware

The payload needs sensors, a controller, a power supply, and a radio.

Software

Onboard software will sample sensors and give each reading a timestamp.

Communication

The ground station receives telemetry and records it locally for playback.

Mechanical Design

The enclosure must fit the payload and hold its components securely in flight.

Testing

A ground test will check the path from a sensor reading to a saved recording.
