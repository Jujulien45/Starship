# Starship | CanSat 2026–2027

## Site Structure

The main navigation is **Home · Tech**.

### Home

1. **Introduction / Hero** — Introduce the team and school with a team or project image, the motto, and a short explanation of the team’s philosophy.
2. **What is CanSat?** — Explain the CanSat challenge and what a CanSat does during flight.
3. **Our Mission** — Describe what Starship plans to do, what the team hopes to learn or demonstrate, and what would count as success.
4. **Meet the Team** — Introduce team members with names, photos, and their roles or contributions.
5. **Sponsors** — Show sponsor logos and links, with a short note about their support where relevant.

### Tech

1. **Data Path** — Open with a diagram of project communications and data flow. A draft flow is sensors → onboard software → radio → ground station → analysis, with an onboard storage branch if applicable. Confirm the actual flow before publishing.
2. **Measurements & Hardware** — Cover the measurements, sensors, and other hardware, including why the team chose them.
3. **Software & Storage** — Explain how readings are collected, processed, stored, and analyzed. Include memory limits if they shaped the design.
4. **Electronics & CAD** — Cover electronics, wiring, and mechanical design. Photos, sketches, or CAD screenshots can fill this section until interactive 3D models are ready.
## Telemetry

The shared header has a distinct Telemetry button at the top right. A separate icon button switches the theme. The dashboard opens with a simulated descent and lets visitors switch to Live.

### Dataset Selector

- **Live** — a live data source and dashboard mode. Before launch, show **Disconnected**.
- **Dummy datasets and recorded flights** — the simulated descent is available now. Recorded flights can be added once flight data is available.

### Shared Dashboard

The dashboard shows data from the selected source.
